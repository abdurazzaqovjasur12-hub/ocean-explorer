Optional recorded audio.

Ocean Explorer makes all of its sound itself (music, waves, bubbles, effects), so this folder can stay empty.

To use your own music instead, put a looping recording here named:

    music.mp3        (MP3 or AAC/M4A work in every browser)

If the file is found it fades in and replaces the generated music. If it is missing or cannot be decoded, the
browser console says exactly why and the generated music keeps playing. Paths are listed in src/audio/files.js.
